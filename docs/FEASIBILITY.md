## Findings (Phase A synthesis, 2026-07-06)

**GATE: PASSED — the program continues.** The gates are clearable at
plausible-but-real edges. Five conclusions, in decreasing importance:

1. **Asymmetry is the lever, not hit rate.** The cheapest honest corners
   are all R_net ≥ 2.0: the anchor cell (zero-50k · 60d · $175 · 1
   trade/day · 2.0R) needs only **WR 45%** → P(pass) 0.74, breach 0.04,
   median 39d, E[cost] $362 (economics-viable). At 1.5R the bar is 52.5%;
   at 1.0R it is 60–65% — the classic high-WR/low-R mean-reversion scalp
   is the HARDEST corner on the map. Family 2 must be built to capture
   **1.5–2R+ net**, e.g. failed-breakout fade targeting the far side of
   the range, not a VWAP scalp.
2. **The economics gate binds harder than G1.** funded_value $1,200 needs
   E[cost] < $600: many G1-clearing cells price at $600–950 →
   GO-RESEARCH. Viable cells cluster at **P(pass) ≥ ~0.65 with median ≤
   ~50 days** — the strategy needs headroom above 0.55, not a graze.
3. **Horizon: 60–90d is the window.** 90d lowers WR bars 2.5–7.5 points
   for selective styles (+30–60% cost); 120d prices itself out almost
   everywhere. The "horizon is purchasable" lever is real but bounded.
4. **The Advanced tier does not help** — 110/144 geometries clear vs
   Zero's 123/144, and its matched-cell WR bars are equal-or-higher
   (bigger target + tighter DD + 50% consistency outweigh no-guard).
   **Zero remains the right tier**; plan-geometry shopping is closed.
   Premium stays excluded (rules unverified).
5. **Risk: $175 stays the anchor.** $250 shaves WR bars but pushes breach
   to 0.15–0.23 (G3-fragile); $125 is safe but slow (E[cost] up ~15–30%).

**Funded-phase coupling (flag for Phase C):** selectivity trades
eval-feasibility against funded value — at 0.33–0.5 trades/day a 5×$200
qualifying cycle stretches to ~4–6 weeks, shrinking funded_value below
$1,200. The family-2 seal must re-derive funded_value at the family's own
cadence; very-low-frequency styles may fail economics even where gates
clear.

**Target specification Family 2 must meet (Phase B screens against
this):** net R **1.5–2.0+** · WR **≥ 45–52%** · **0.5–1 trade/day** ·
median pass ≤ ~50 days · on the verified Zero tier at $175 risk,
horizon 60–90d.
