# Verdict #1 — 2026-07-06 — NO-GO (VOID FOR INSTRUMENT DEFECT)

**Protocol:** `d3fa96c01bbab50a` (PREREG sealed 2026-07-06, git blob

## The result as computed

NO-GO — both instruments failed independently at the OOS gates.

## Why it is void, with evidence

Every one of the 128 cells, on every fold (dev/OOS/lockbox), on both
instruments, returned **p_pass = 0.000 AND p_breach = 0.000** with median
days = the full 60-day horizon. All five null seeds: 0.000. That is not
"no edge" — with $175 risk/trade actually deployed, the two-barrier
geometry makes a literally-zero breach rate across ~2,000 Monte-Carlo
starts impossible. It is the signature of **zero trades ever occurring**.

Root cause (verified numerically on the real data):

- The loader computes **daily** ATR. Real MES daily ATR: median **55.1
  pts** (min 16.5, max 242.5).
- The sealed grid's stop axis (0.75–1.5 × ATR) therefore priced one micro
  contract at **$213–423** true risk.
- `risk_usd` = $175 → `int(175 // 213…423)` = **0 contracts** → NoTrade,
  every day since 2019, in every cell — including the null (same sizing).
- The positive control never caught it because the synthetic world's ATR
  is **8.0 pts** (`synth.py`): stops ≈ 6 pts ≈ $34/contract ≈ 5 contracts.
  The harness was validated in a volatility regime ~7× smaller than
  reality. The design intent (CONTEXT §6 ruin math: $150–200 risk) always
  assumed intraday-scale stops of ~10–20 pts.

## What was and was not learned

- **Learned:** the sealed protocol carried a latent units defect — the
  stop axis is daily-ATR-scaled while the risk budget is intraday-scaled —
  and the harness lacked a "did anything actually trade" validity
  assertion. Silence read as survival.
- **NOT learned:** anything about market edge. Zero trades were simulated;
  every statistic is a structural zero. The lockbox returned all-zeros
  from no trades — **no market information was revealed**, which is what
  makes a clean v2 legitimate rather than gate-shopping.

## Disposition

Verdict #1 stands in the record as **void for instrument defect** (the
analogue of a lab assay with a dead reagent). Per PREREG: departures
require a new dated PREREG version and a re-run. PREREG v2 must:

1. Re-parameterize the stop axis in ORB-native units (multiples of the
   opening-range height, not daily ATR) so sizing and the ruin math are
   dimensionally consistent.
2. Add a fail-loud validity gate to the sweep: a fold whose total trade
   count is zero (or below a floor) aborts the run — instrument failure,
   never a NO-GO.
3. Re-run the positive control at a realistic ATR scale (~55 pts) before
   sealing, plus a dev-fold-only smoke check on real data (trades occur,
   contract counts 1–3) — instrumentation validity only; OOS and lockbox
   stay unread.
