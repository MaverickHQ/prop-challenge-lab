# Verdict #2 — 2026-07-06 — **NO-GO (validated, final)**

**Protocol:** v2, `12ca0a32f180648d` (git blob `e2fa9aa4…` in
PREREG.sealed) · **Runtime:** 699 s · **Data:** MES/MNQ continuous 1-min,
1,741 tradable days each (2019-05-24 → 2026-07-02) · **Instrument health:**
validity gates armed and silent — strategies traded 78–86% of days at 1–12

## Decision

**NO-GO on both instruments, independently, at the OOS gates.** Per PREREG
§8: validated no-go — **do not pay the fee.** This document is the write-up.

## The numbers (full detail: `scripts/postmortem.py`, deterministic)

Gates: G1 P(pass) ≥ 0.55 · G2 ≥ null + 0.15 · G3 P(breach) ≤ 0.30 ·
G4 plateau 5 @ 0.05. 128 cells per instrument per fold.

| Fold | MES best P(pass) | MNQ best P(pass) | null | cells ≥ G1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| dev (in-sample!) | 0.031 | 0.042 | 0.000 | 0 / 128 |
| OOS | 0.187 | 0.128 | 0.000 | 0 / 128 |
| lockbox | 0.045 | 0.072 | 0.000 | 0 / 128 |

- **The miss is an order of magnitude, everywhere.** The single best cell
  in the entire experiment (MES OOS, 0.187) sits at a third of G1 — and
  carries P(breach) 0.519, failing G3 outright. The best G3-clean cell
  (0.163) still misses G1 by 3.4×.
- **The family never worked even in-sample.** Dev-fold bests are 0.03–0.04.
  This is not an overfit edge dying out-of-sample — there was never
  anything to overfit. The failure is broad, not narrow: no plateau, no
  instrument, no range window, no filter setting comes close.
- **The null's P(pass) is 0.000 while actively trading** — random ORB-style
  entries at $175 risk, ≤2 trades/day, essentially never reach +$3,000
  inside 60 trading days. The theoretical ~30% "geometric floor" from the
  two-barrier sketch does not survive contact with real intraday costs and
  cadence: at this design there is no meaningful pass-by-luck channel.
  (Corollary: any vendor of "pass rates" at this cadence is selling either
  size, risk, or fiction.)
- MES OOS shows 2/128 cells above null+0.15 — with null at zero that is
  just "2 cells above 0.15," both G1/G3 failures, no plateau: noise on a
  128-cell grid.

## Interpretation

1. **The arithmetic that had to be true wasn't.** Passing needs ≈ +$50/day
   net for 60 days. At our sizing that demands a sustained ~55%+ win rate
   at 1.5R on breakouts after $5/RT costs. The measurement says the ORB
   family on MES/MNQ delivers nowhere near it — in any fold, era, or
   parameterization.
2. **This independently confirms the published evidence** (EVIDENCE.md §3:
   unfiltered ORB backtests show no edge after costs; only filtered
   variants retain any). Our one allowed filter (VWAP side) was not enough.
3. **The conservative fill model biases P(pass) down** (stop-before-target,
   1-tick slippage each side) — but conservatism explains percentage
   points, not the 10× gap to the bar. This is not a modeling artifact.
4. **The base rates were the tell.** Population per-attempt pass ≈ 16.8%,
   most failures drawdown breaches, and the paid tail ≈ 5–7% of buyers
   (EVIDENCE.md §1). A design honest enough to refuse to overfit lands
   where the population lands: below the bar, without paying to learn it.

## What would change the answer (for any future PREREG v3+)

Only a **different strategy family** or **different economics** — not more
parameters on this one. The failure is family-level: (a) a signal with
genuinely higher per-trade expectancy after costs, or (b) a materially
different risk geometry (which the ruin math constrains hard at $2,000
trailing). Any such attempt is a new dated protocol with its own controls;
grid-extending v2 would be exactly the sweep-until-it-passes failure this
repo exists to refuse.
